---
name: External link fidelity
description: Verify user-supplied external destinations after delegated visual builds.
---

Delegated design passes can replace exact external destinations with plausible placeholder URLs, even when the requested links are in the brief.

**Why:** A portfolio archive can look finished while quietly sending readers to the wrong publication or section.

**How to apply:** Before delivery, grep the final content source for every user-supplied URL and confirm card titles and summaries describe those destinations.
